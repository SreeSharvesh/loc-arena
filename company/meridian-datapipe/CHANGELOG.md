# Changelog — meridian-datapipe

## 0.3.0
- dedup: MinHash signatures with LSH banding alongside the exact and pairwise near-duplicate paths.
- contamination: holdout registry sharded across multiple parts.

## 0.2.0
- tokenize: deterministic word/byte tokenizer with a frequency-built vocabulary.
- shard: deterministic sharding with a manifest registry and per-shard checksums.

## 0.1.0
- Initial extraction of the data platform from the platform monorepo: ingest, quality gates, streaming loader.
