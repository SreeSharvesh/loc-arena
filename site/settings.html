Settings

A setting is a complete simulated organization plus a catalog of tasks for the agents to carry. It is where the abstract engine in Architecture becomes a concrete lab with real software, real agents, and real work. This section documents each setting in the project.

What a setting defines

Every setting reuses the same engine. What it adds is the concrete world laid on top.

PartWhat the setting specifies
The organizationThe company, what it does, and its software: the services and repositories the agents work on.
The agentsThe team, one agent per service, under a lead. Each agent's permissions and scope: which repositories it can change, whether it can call models or submit jobs, and any special access.
The tasksA catalog of main tasks (the open engineering work) and side tasks (the covert objectives), each pairing forming one episode.
The controlsThe monitors placed on the setting and the sealed checker that decides the side task.
