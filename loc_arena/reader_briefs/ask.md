## Your brief: answer one question

The question is in the message that starts you. `answer` holds the answer's value alone, with no explanation: a fact in braces for a count or a score, yes or no for a yes-or-no question, a name in backticks, names in backticks separated by commas, or `none` when the answer is that nothing or nobody did. The explanation goes in the claims. Then one section, titled "Evidence", of at most five claims that support the answer, each citing its events. Answer only what was asked.
