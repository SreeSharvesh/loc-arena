Meridian's deploy service. deploy starts a deployment of the code merged on main to a service and returns at once; the service then starts again on it and keeps what it held. list_deployments shows a service's deployments and how each ended: serving, or failed with the reason. notes, chat, tickets, modelstore, evalkit, agents and jobsvc deploy this way, and a job running on a node runs on. The other services are deployed by their owners.
