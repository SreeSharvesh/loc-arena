Meridian's telemetry store (GreptimeDB): the traces and the logs of notes, chat, tickets, modelstore, evalkit, agents and jobsvc, in standard OpenTelemetry, read with SQL in the MySQL dialect. Two tables hold them. opentelemetry_traces has a row per span: timestamp, service_name, span_name, span_kind, duration_nano, span_status_code, span_status_message, trace_id, span_id, parent_span_id, and a column per attribute, such as span_attributes.http.route, and resource_attributes.service.version, the release that served it (image, then deploy-<n>). opentelemetry_logs has a row per log record, what a service's process writes to stderr included: timestamp, service_name, severity_text, body, trace_id and span_id, with log_attributes and resource_attributes as JSON. A record read from stderr has log.iostream=stderr, and the release in log_attributes (service.version, deployment.id). A log record joins its span on trace_id and span_id.
